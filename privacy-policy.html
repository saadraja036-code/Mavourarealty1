Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Mavoura Realty LLC ("Company," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when visiting our website or contacting us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website or contact us, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Property interests and preferences
  • Messages and inquiries you submit

We may also collect certain technical information, such as browser type, device information, and website usage information, if applicable.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected to:

  • Respond to your questions and inquiries.
  • Discuss real estate services and opportunities.
  • Communicate with you about your requests.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Maintain website security and prevent misuse.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Contact Form Submissions

The contact form currently displayed on this website is a demonstration form. Submitting it displays a confirmation message but does not transmit or store your information.

If the contact form is connected to an external submission service in the future, this policy will be updated to describe the service and how it handles submitted information.

4. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies if these features are enabled. These technologies may help improve website performance and understand how visitors use our website.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

If we collect personal information, we may share it with service providers who help operate our website or provide business services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

6. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or use third-party services. These services have their own privacy policies and practices.

Mavoura Realty LLC is not responsible for the privacy practices of websites or services that we do not operate.

7. Email Communications

If you contact us by email, we may use your email address to respond to your request and communicate about the services you requested.

8. SMS and Text Message Communications

If you separately provide consent to receive text messages from Mavoura Realty LLC, we may use your phone number to send messages related to your inquiry or requested services.

  • Message frequency may vary.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.
  • For assistance, reply HELP.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any service.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. Information may be shared with service providers as necessary to deliver messaging services.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information provided to us. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information.

To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the email address listed below.

12. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
